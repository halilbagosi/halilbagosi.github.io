Snippets

A free Mac app for keeping, finding and live-previewing the code you reuse. Everything stays on your Mac.

Requires macOS 26 or later.

Organize

Nested collections, language filters, attachments and fast search across your whole library.

Preview live

Run HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, GLSL, Metal shaders and Swift right next to the code.

Copy from anywhere

Search and copy a snippet from the menu bar without leaving the app you’re working in.

Save as you copy

Optionally, Snippets offers to save code you copy elsewhere as a new snippet.

Shortcuts and Siri

Create, find, copy and open snippets from your own shortcuts.

Private by design

No accounts, analytics or tracking. Your library never leaves your Mac.
